Project 01 · guidance stack

GPS-denied quadcopter guidance

Companion computer owns pose, map, and plan. PX4 on the FMU owns attitude, mixing, and failsafes. First airframe is a PX4 quadcopter. Interfaces (vision pose, occupancy, setpoints) do not care if mixing later becomes Quadplane/VTOL.

Bring-up sequence (the demo I would run)

1 SITLPX4 + Gazebo, same ROS graph as hardware.
2 GPS OFFLock vision pose quality. Compare EKF vs VIO in bag.
3 MAPShort corridor, occupancy inflation = vehicle radius.
4 PLANWaypoint in map frame, local avoid, then RTL.
5 ORINSame nodes on Jetson, MAVSDK offboard to real FMU.

Why Quadplane/VTOL does not fork this stack

SLAM, occupancy, and the planner live in the map frame. PX4 mixing and VTOL mode live on the FMU. Switching airframe is SYS_AUTOSTART + mixer, not a new VIO. That matches Hest’s “start on quadcopter, architecture must scale to Quadplane/VTOL”.
