RTAB-Map dense SLAM at 30 FPS

Client needed a proprietary 3D scanning library after ARDK 2.0 was deprecated. I customized RTAB-Map (C++) on iPhone LiDAR + RGB. The output is a dense point cloud in real time — a mapping frontend, not a detector.
